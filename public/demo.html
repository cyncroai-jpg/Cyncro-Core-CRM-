Q1
What are your primary business goals for the next 6 months?
We want to generate 40+ leads per month from Google and close 20 of them. We're expanding to a 4th location in Plano in Q1 2027 and need our systems ready.
Q2
What is your current monthly marketing budget and where is it spent?
About $3,500/mo — split between Google Ads ($2,000) and Yelp ads ($1,500). We haven't tracked ROI before. No Facebook yet.
Q3
How do leads currently contact you and what's your response process?
Mostly phone calls and the website contact form. We check the form once a day. Calls go to the office line and often get missed after 5 PM.
Q4
Who are your main competitors in your area?
Top Choice Roofing and Dallas Pro Roofing are the big ones. Top Choice spends heavily on Google. We want to outrank them.
Q5
What does your ideal customer look like?
Homeowners in Frisco, Allen, McKinney — homes built 2005–2015 in the $350K–$600K range. Insurance claims and storm damage are our bread and butter.
